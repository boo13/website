# Tweak Pane
**Reference:** Inspired by sandbox.html control-panel pattern
**What's being tested:** Not a variant itself — a tool for fine-tuning the chosen direction once one of the three variants is shortlisted.
**Verdict:** tool (not subject to shortlist/reject)

## Notes
- Load a preset first, then adjust individual levers.
- Click "Apply & Reset Scroll" to rebuild the ScrollTrigger with new values.
- When you find a config that feels right, copy the values to the winning variant's `DECISIONS.md` and into `src/sections/hero.js`.
