# Hero Aperture Dual Video

**What is being tested:** The aperture transition with a second work-sample video resolving behind the About copy while the original montage remains present as a blurred perimeter.

**Key choices**

- Reuse the same pinned aperture timeline as `hero-aperture`.
- Keep the center treatment black-only so text contrast does not depend on a white glow.
- Bring the second video from heavy blur to near-focus as the About text fills.
- Reduce the original montage edge opacity so the two video layers do not compete.

**Watch for**

- If the second video distracts from the text, darken the radial overlay before lowering video opacity.
- If the transition feels too busy, delay the second video focus until after the aperture reaches half size.
