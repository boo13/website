Experiments

Hero → About Transition · Round 1

Variant 1 · Baseline

Restrained

Current production config. Subtle zoom-through — hero scales 1.15× while the video softens to 8px blur. Short scroll window, fast scrub.

scale 1.15× blur 8px scroll 150% scrub 1.5
Variant 2 · v2 Port

Aggressive

Ported from bd9f06e v2/zoom-transition.js. High-impact zoom — hero rushes forward at 2.5× while the video hits 20px blur. About emerges from the fog.

scale 2.5× blur 20px scroll 200% scrub 1.2
Variant 3 · v4 Inspired

Z-Depth

Inspired by 0cc8d3b v4/CinematicZoom.js. Very high scrub (4×) means the camera moves in slow, deliberate pulls. Rack-focus blur of 18px. Long scroll window gives it a film-edit feel.

scale 1.25× blur 18px scroll 300% scrub 4.0
Variant 4 · Portal

Through About

The About layer sits behind the hero in a pinned scene. The camera pushes through the landing page instead of scrolling down to the next section.

pin 240% scale 3.35× depth layer focus pull
Variant 5 · Aperture

Video Edges

Keeps the montage playing as a blurred perimeter while a center aperture opens onto the About section with black radial separation and text fill.

pin 260% radial mask edge video text fill
Variant 6 · Aperture

Dual Video

Adds a second work-sample video behind the aperture. It resolves from blur as the About copy fills from dim to full opacity.
