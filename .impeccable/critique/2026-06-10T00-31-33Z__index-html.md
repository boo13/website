## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Not AI slop. The deterministic detector found only trivia (Arial in the inline preloader styles — intentional pre-font-load). The concept — title-sequence preloader, aperture iris hero, credits-roll work section — is distinctive and domain-true. No gradient text, no card-grid template, no eyebrow scaffolding (one "About" label total). The risk here is not genericness; it is that the page's quality is hostage to video loading and that its pacing sags after the gallery.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] The entire visual identity is hostage to two videos.** Hero + aperture are black voids until (unless) video arrives — slow connections, iOS Low Power Mode (autoplay blocked), data-saver, or CDN hiccups all produce a near-empty first screen. The static state needs to be a designed composition (poster frames, stills), not an absence.
2. **[P1] The Work section is a 25-row monotone wall — the longest stretch of the page.** Emotional flatline right after the gallery peak; the page ends on its weakest three viewports. Curate into tiers (selected credits + expandable archive), group, or give a few rows imagery.
3. **[P1] No human being on the page.** No portrait, and the bio is two sentences embedded mid-animation in the hero scrub. No standalone About beat for a portfolio whose product is a person.
4. **[P2] CTA beat is generic and inverted.** "Stay in the loop" newsletter is the primary action; "Get in Touch" is buried under a divider. For a hire-me portfolio the conversion hierarchy is backwards, and the section is visually the sparsest on the page (peak-end problem).
5. **[P2] Mobile is a degraded, nav-less experience.** `.pill-nav { display:none }` ≤768px with no replacement; left-anchored hero composition and dead-black card areas read emptier on a phone.
6. **[P2] Zero section headings + whisper-quiet utility text costs orientation.** Section titles are commented out; nav links at 0.4-alpha 11px uppercase are near-invisible. One more hierarchy level between the name and the rows is missing.

## What's Working

- Coherent, ownable concept: every device (timecode, corners, iris, credits inversion, light/dark flip) comes from filmmaking. This is voice, not decoration.
- Disciplined system: two typefaces, opacity-ramp text hierarchy, OKLCH tokens, a named easing palette. Rare even in professional work.
- The Work section's light inversion is the page's one big tonal beat and it lands.

## Questions to Consider

- What should a visitor do 60 seconds in: watch a reel, or email Randy? The page never decides.
- If video never loads, is the page still a portfolio?
- Could the Work list be a sales tool (curated 8) instead of a database (25)?
