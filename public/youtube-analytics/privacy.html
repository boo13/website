Privacy Policy

YouTube Analytics Collector · last updated 15 September 2026

This policy covers the private, read-only YouTube Analytics collector operated by Randy Counsman on a personal Google Cloud project. It is not a public app, not an Every product, and not affiliated branding for Every.

What is accessed

With consent, the collector may request read-only Google API access limited to:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — only if revenue collection is explicitly opted into

Browser consent is completed by Randy Counsman as himself, for a channel he is authorized to read.

Why

The data is used only to pull YouTube Analytics into a personal production dashboard. The collector does not upload videos, moderate comments, change ads or channel settings, manage the Google account, or act as a service account.

Where it is stored

OAuth tokens and analytics data stay in local private storage on Randy’s own computers. They are kept outside git and are not uploaded to a public bucket or shared hosting.

What is not done with the data

The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with a third party.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions (third-party access) and from YouTube’s third-party access settings. After revocation, the collector can no longer use previously granted tokens.
