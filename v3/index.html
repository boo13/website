About

Documentary producer focused on stories that explore the pivotal moments where history, commerce, and human ambition intersect.

Over fifteen years bringing complex narratives to life across Netflix, History, PBS, and AMC. Each project begins with deep archival research and ends with storytelling that resonates—whether it's American frontier justice, industrial titans, or revolutionary turning points.

The work lives at the crossroads of three fascinations: the sweep of history, the mechanics of business and power, and the innovations that reshape society. This convergence creates documentaries that don't just inform—they captivate.
