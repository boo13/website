15+ Years

Creating Premium Documentary Content

Documentary producer with a passion for stories that sit at the crossroads of history and human character.

From archival research to scripted recreations, every project gets the same care—whether it's Wyatt Earp and the Cowboy War for Netflix, The Men Who Built America for History, or Sons of Liberty. The process thrives on tight deadlines, creative problem-solving, and keeping everyone aligned from first pitch to final delivery.

The goal is always the same: take complex historical narratives and turn them into compelling television that audiences actually want to watch.
